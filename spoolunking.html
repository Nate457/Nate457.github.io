Lore

Deep beneath the Woolworks, the ancient dungeons stir. Thred — last of the Yarn Weavers — descends into the dark in search of the Spool of Origins, stolen by the Wine Wraiths.

The caves are alive. The walls remember. The slimes multiply. The cats... are just cats. Vicious, unhinged cats.

Three tiers await: Upper Caverns, Sunken Vaults, and The Final Tangle — where the boss waits.
