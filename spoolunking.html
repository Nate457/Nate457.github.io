Lore

Thred — last of the Yarn Weavers — descends into the ancient dungeons of the Woolworks to reclaim the Spool of Origins, stolen by the Wine King.

Three areas of increasing darkness await: The Tangled Cavern, The Wool Labyrinth, and The Spool Chamber — where the Wine King holds court.

Collect thread from fallen enemies. Between levels, visit the Skill Tree to grow stronger. Your progression carries through every run.
